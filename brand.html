04 — Motion

Breathing, not blinking.

Motion in Homming is slow, organic, and almost imperceptible. The gradient drifts. The mark breathes. Nothing snaps.

Principles

  • Drift — gradients ease 8–16s, never loop harshly.
  • Breathe — marks pulse 4–5s at ±6% scale.
  • Fade — text arrives in 800–1200ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1).
  • Respect — honor prefers-reduced-motion.
05 — Voice

Quiet confidence.

We speak in short, considered sentences. We use "you" more than "we." We never oversell. We invite instead of announce.

Sounds like us

"Something quietly in the making."

"Designed for the relationships you keep."

"A calmer way to stay close."

Short, warm, a little poetic. Never explaining more than needed.
Doesn't sound like us

"Revolutionary AI-powered relationship platform."

"Unlock deeper connections. 10× your bonds."

"The future of human interaction is here."

Superlatives, buzzwords, growth-hack cadence — none of it.
